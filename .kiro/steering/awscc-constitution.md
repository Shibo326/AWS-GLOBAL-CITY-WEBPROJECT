# AWS Cloud Club — STI Global City: Constitution & By-Laws

## Organization Identity

- **Full Name:** AWS Cloud Club – Systems Technology Institute Global City Taguig
- **Acronyms:** AWS Cloud Club – STI Global City, AWSCC – STI BGC
- **Type:** Special-interest, non-profit, non-political, non-sectarian student organization
- **Location:** STI Academic Center, University Parkway Drive, Bonifacio Global City, Taguig, 1634 Metro Manila, Philippines
- **Affiliations:** AWS User Group Philippines, AWS Academic Advocacy
- **Mascot:** "Rory" — the official club mascot

---

## Vision

A long-standing community of passionate learners from various universities across the country who embrace innovation and leverage the power of Amazon Web Services to drive technological advancements.

## Mission

Committed to community service initiatives and developing well-rounded perspectives on contributing to society. Empowering students to specialize in their desired field through inclusive educational initiatives, interactive workshops, and networking avenues. Equipping students with the tools to thrive in the digital economy and contribute to the transformation of industries through AWS.

## Goals

1. **Education and Skill Development** — Workshops, seminars, and training sessions on AWS services, architecture, and best practices
2. **Community Building Across the Country** — Regular meetings, events, online forums; assisting other communities in establishing university chapters
3. **Industry Engagement** — Guest lectures, industry visits, internships; bridging academia and industry
4. **Innovation and Projects** — Encouraging AWS-powered projects solving real-world challenges
5. **Student-Centric** — Prioritizing student needs, growth, and success through tailored programs and resources

---

## Basic Principles

1. Authority and power lie with members; all governing bodies derived from them
2. Main objective: services enhancing member development in their fields
3. Dedicated to promoting human values and virtues; intellectual, social, and moral well-being
4. Affiliated with AWS User Group Philippines and AWS Academic Advocacy; adheres to AWS Code of Conduct
5. Upholding good citizenship and civic responsibility
6. Cultivating positive relationships between society and the wider STI community
7. All members must follow rules in STI Student Handbook

## Membership

### Eligibility
- Open to any bona fide student within STI
- No discrimination based on race, religion, national origin, ethnicity, color, age, gender, gender identity, marital status, citizenship, sexual orientation, or disability

### Requirements
- Duly signed membership form or electronic form (via Microsoft Forms)
- Softcopy of latest Registration Form from STI-Global
- 2x2 picture taken within 6 months

### Termination Conditions
- Did not participate in at least one (1) event within the semester
- Finished program or graduated
- Behavior negatively affecting organization integrity
- Wishes to terminate role within Skill Builder Departments or Office
- Failed to submit membership revalidation forms or abide by minimum requirements
- Breach of Contract (NDA, dishonesty in submissions, dishonesty in credentials)

### Revalidation Requirements (Per Semester)
- Certifications, Badges, and Completed Projects (Skill Builder Activities)
- Community Engagements (AWS User Group Participations)
- University Chapter Engagements (AWS Cloud Club Event Participation)

### Certificate of Membership
- Given after completion of first semester or upon revalidation approval
- Requires LinkedIn account creation and joining AWS Cloud Clubs Philippines Regional Meetup
- Can be revoked for negligence, misconduct, non-compliance, breach of confidentiality, insubordination, harm to reputation, violation of policies, failure to fulfill obligations

## Renewal & Succession

### Board of Executives
- Renewal held in April
- Registration and selection of new/vacant positions in May
- Renewal approved by anonymous two-thirds vote (last week of April)
- Outgoing officers must train successors
- Withdrawn successors barred from office for following academic year

### University Captain & Co-Captain
- If Captain doesn't renew, Board nominates candidate (two-thirds vote required)
- Must undergo AWS Cloud Clubs application process with Academic Advocacy approval
- If CEO position vacant, Co-Captain completes remainder of term

### Position Switching
- Officers wanting different office must not renew in April, wait for May registration
- Within same office: requires contract agreement with both signatures + Board approval
- Not applicable within Executive Office and Finance Office

### Skill Builder Department
- Renewal held 2 weeks after last event of current term
- New selection follows renewal process

### BuildHers+ Ambassadors
- Renewal held 2 weeks after last event of current term
- New appointment approved by Board and supported by AWS User Group BuildHers+

## Key Policies

### Non-Hazing Clause
STI prohibits any hazing activity — defined as any planned or spontaneous activity that is demeaning, produces ridicule/harassment, causes physical/mental duress, or threatens health/safety. Offenses investigated immediately with suspension penalties.

### Indebtedness
No debt shall be incurred by this organization.

### Removal from Office
- CEO assesses charges, conducts preliminary investigation
- Any member may initiate removal via petition with signatures from all voting members
- Executive Board notifies officer and arranges vote within 7 days
- Failure to confirm attendance within 5 days = grounds for removal
- Members notified at least 1 week before removal vote
- Removal requires two-thirds (2/3) vote of all present voting members

### Amendments and Revisions
- Proposed by two-thirds vote of active members
- Must be presented in writing 10 days prior to consideration
- Valid when ratified by majority vote in club referendum (within 1 month of approval)
